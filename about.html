About Karnataka Tourism

Karnataka is a land of ancient heritage, vibrant culture, and breathtaking landscapes.

From world-famous monuments to serene beaches and coffee plantations, Karnataka is a traveler’s paradise.

The state is home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites, royal palaces, lush forests, and majestic waterfalls.

It offers a unique blend of tradition, history, adventure, and natural beauty.

Here are some of the must-visit destinations in Karnataka:

Top Tourist Destinations

Hampi

Hampi, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, was once the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire.

It is famous for its ancient temples, stone chariots, and sprawling ruins.

Virupaksha Temple and Vittala Temple are architectural marvels here.

The boulder-strewn landscapes add to its otherworldly charm.

A paradise for history buffs and photographers alike.

Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is one of the most magnificent royal palaces in India.

Built in Indo-Saracenic style, it features domes, arches, and stained glass windows.

The palace glows with thousands of lights during the Dussehra festival.

Inside, its ornate halls display paintings, jewelry, and artifacts.

It is the pride of Mysore and a cultural icon of Karnataka.

Jog Falls

Jog Falls, located in Shivamogga district, is among India’s tallest waterfalls.

It cascades in four distinct streams: Raja, Rani, Roarer, and Rocket.

During the monsoon, it transforms into a thunderous spectacle.

Surrounding viewpoints provide breathtaking views of the falls.

A must-visit for nature lovers and photography enthusiasts.

Coorg

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is Karnataka’s coffee capital.

It is surrounded by misty hills, lush greenery, and coffee plantations.

Abbey Falls and Raja’s Seat are popular attractions here.

The region is also famous for its unique Kodava culture and cuisine.

A peaceful getaway often called the “Scotland of India.”

Gokarna

Gokarna is a serene coastal town known for its pristine beaches.

Om Beach, shaped like the Om symbol, is a major attraction.

The town also has ancient temples with spiritual significance.

It’s a favorite spot for backpackers and adventure seekers.

Gokarna blends relaxation, spirituality, and natural beauty.
